# Macro Trade Idea – Pay Fixed on 10-Year USD Interest Rate Swap

## Overview

This project presents a structured macro trade idea recommending a **pay-fixed position in the 10-year USD Interest Rate Swap (IRS)**. The idea is based on a **higher-for-longer interest rate environment** following the latest FOMC meeting. The write-up simulates a real-world sales & trading desk deliverable aimed at institutional clients or internal MD review.

## Objective

To express a tactical rates view that leverages:
- Persistent inflation pressures
- Elevated Fed rate path projections (dot plot)
- Upward pressure on the term premium
- A desire to position for curve steepening in the medium term

## Trade Summary

- **Trade:** Pay fixed on 10-year USD IRS
- **View:** Medium- to long-end rates will rise further as markets price in sustained high Fed policy rates and sticky inflation
- **Rationale:**
  - Fed signaling one more hike and elevated longer-run policy rate
  - Sticky inflation (5Y breakeven ~2.48%)
  - Term premium likely to rise amid uncertainty and duration demand
  - 10-year point offers attractive duration exposure with manageable carry
